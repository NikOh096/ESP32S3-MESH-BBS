READY WHEN YOU ARE

Connect your board

Close any serial monitor or IDE connection first. Your browser will ask which USB port to use.

Installing replaces the BBS firmware. Existing MESHBBS bulletins and owner pairing are kept. Nothing is uploaded from your board.

Your board is ready for setup.

  1. Install the Android app.
  2. Tap BOOT three times, then hold it for three seconds and release. Finish within 12 seconds.
  3. Open Devices in the app, find your board, then choose your mesh radio.

The app handles the radio's displayed or fixed PIN. Your phone can stay connected while the BBS runs.

Need a hand connecting?

Use desktop Chrome or Edge over HTTPS or localhost. A charging-only cable will not work.

If the USB port does not appear, hold BOOT, press and release RST, then release BOOT. Try Connect again. This starts the bootloader; it is different from the app's pairing sequence.

If an install is interrupted, keep the board plugged in, enter the bootloader again and repeat the install. Do not erase it to retry.

Only the listed ESP32-S3 boards with 8 MB octal PSRAM are supported in this release. The installer checks the chip and memory, but it cannot determine your LED wiring.
